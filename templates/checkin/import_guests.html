Spreadsheet Format Guide

Your Excel file should have a header row. The importer auto-detects column names — these are all accepted:

Name *
Name, Full Name, Guest Name, Attendee
Email (optional)
Email, Email Address, E-mail, Mail
Phone (optional)
Phone, Phone Number, Mobile, Tel

Example spreadsheet layout:

Name Email Phone
John Adeyemi john@example.com 08012345678
Amaka Obi amaka@example.com 08098765432
Tunde Bello 07011223344

Guests without an email address will still be imported and get a QR code — they just won't receive a confirmation email. Duplicate emails are automatically skipped.
